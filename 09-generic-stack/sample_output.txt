=== Generic Stack tested with three data types ===

--- Stack<int> ---
Initially empty? true
pushed 10 | size = 1
pushed 20 | size = 2
pushed 30 | size = 3
top = 30
popping 30
popping 20
popping 10
Empty after popping all? true
Error handled: pop() called on an empty stack

Why templates help: one class definition serves int, float and
std::string. The compiler generates each version when it is used.
